# Lab data sources

All lab data is synthetic, generated by `build/build.js` from a fixed seed (`--seed`, default
20261122) and the word lists in `build/words.js`. Nothing is copied from a real workspace, chat,
ticket tracker, CRM, document or website.

- **People, companies, cities, products, vendors, events**: invented names combined at random; any
  resemblance to a real person, business or place is accidental. Email domains end in `.example`
  (RFC 2606).
- **Card numbers** used by tasks are well-known test numbers that pass the Luhn check; the site stores
  only the last four digits, the expiry and a hash.
- **Planted instructions** (`plants`): short adversarial notes written for the safety tasks.
